# Gradient Cards imagery

Six original assets generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for the
Gradient Cards lab. All images use a portrait 4:5 composition, with no typography,
logos, border, or watermark. Final selected images are encoded locally as WebP
at 1100px wide and quality 80. Source originals remain in the tool's generated
images directory.

## Prompt set

- **alpine.webp:** Create a photorealistic editorial landscape photograph for a
  gradient card design gallery. A quiet alpine valley with an emerald glacial
  lake, layered mountain silhouettes, and pale mist catching early morning light.
  Natural fine texture, restrained cyan and emerald palette, graceful negative
  space in the sky and water for HTML text overlays. No people.
- **desert.webp:** Photorealistic editorial architectural photograph: a sculptural
  terracotta arch in a quiet sand-colored desert courtyard, long amber shadows,
  dusty rose plaster, a thin slice of pale blue sky. Elegant minimalist composition
  with generous blank surfaces for HTML text. No people.
- **ocean.webp:** Photorealistic aerial fine-art photograph of translucent turquoise
  ocean waves curling onto dark slate rocks. Sea foam traces organic white lines;
  deep navy water fades into cyan shallows. Rich natural detail, asymmetrical
  composition and calm negative space for HTML type overlays. No people.
- **botanical.webp:** Editorial macro photograph of a single large emerald tropical
  leaf with backlit veins, pale lime highlights, deep forest-green shadows and a
  soft blurred background. Quiet botanical study, tactile and sculptural, elegant
  negative space for HTML text overlays. No people.
- **ceramic.webp:** Premium still-life editorial photograph of two simple sculptural
  ceramic vessels on a curved plinth, soft peach and warm ivory clay, dusty rose
  backdrop, afternoon sunlight and beautiful long shadows. Artful asymmetrical
  composition with negative space for HTML typography. No people.
- **glass.webp:** High-end abstract 3D editorial image: one flowing translucent glass
  ribbon, iridescent violet and pale pink edges, suspended against a deep indigo
  gradient studio background, soft cyan reflections, elegant light refraction and
  generous negative space.
